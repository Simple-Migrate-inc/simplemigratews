**Effective date: October 29, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Simplemigrate Inc. ("Simple Migrate", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you visit simplemigrate.com, contact us, or use the Simple Migrate client dashboard. We follow Canadian privacy law, including the _Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act_ (PIPEDA) and applicable provincial laws such as British Columbia's _Personal Information Protection Act_ and Quebec's _Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector_.

### Who is responsible

Our Privacy Officer is responsible for how we handle personal information. You can contact them at **[simplemigratecom@gmail.com](mailto:simplemigratecom@gmail.com)** or by mail at **1055 W Georgia St, Vancouver, BC V6E 3P2**.

### Information we collect

**Information you give us**

- Contact details, such as your name, email address and phone number, when you contact us, book a call or subscribe to our newsletter.
- If you become a client: information about your goals, work and business experience, finances, family and immigration history, and the documents you upload to your readiness file.
- Information about businesses you are interested in buying or selling.

**Information from others, with your permission**

- Updates from licensed professionals, partners or real estate agents you have chosen to work with through us.

**Information collected automatically, with your consent**

- How you use our website (pages visited, clicks, scrolling, device and browser type, approximate location based on IP address), collected through Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity only if you accept analytics cookies. See "Cookies and analytics" below.

### How we use personal information

- To respond to your enquiries and provide the services you ask for.
- To prepare and organize your readiness file and introduce you to licensed professionals, partners and real estate agents you approve.
- To manage your account, keep it secure and keep a record of activity on your file.
- To understand how our website is used and improve it (only with your consent to analytics cookies).
- To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

Simple Migrate organizes information and makes introductions. We do not decide immigration eligibility; that is decided by the relevant program and confirmed by a licensed professional.

### Consent

We collect, use and share personal information with your consent, except where the law allows otherwise. You can withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits, by contacting our Privacy Officer. Withdrawing consent may affect our ability to provide some services.

### When we share information

- **Licensed professionals, partners and real estate agents** — only after you approve a specific, time-limited share, and only the information covered by that share. You can revoke a share at any time.
- **Service providers** who help us run our services, such as website and application hosting, database hosting, email delivery and analytics. They may only use the information to provide services to us.
- **When required by law**, for example in response to a valid legal request.

### AI tools

AI tools may help organize your file under the supervision of a licensed professional. Your documents are never used to train AI models.

### Where information is stored

Client dashboard data, including uploaded documents, is stored in Canada (Montréal region). Some of our service providers, including Google and Microsoft for website analytics, may process information outside Canada, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

### Cookies and analytics

**Essential cookies and storage.** If you sign in to the client dashboard, we use cookies that are necessary to keep you signed in and to protect your account. We also store your cookie choice in your browser so we don't ask you again. These cannot be turned off without breaking those features.

**Analytics cookies (optional).** With your consent, we use:

- **Google Analytics 4** (Google LLC) to measure visits and how our pages are used. Cookies: `_ga` and `_ga_<ID>` (up to 2 years).
- **Microsoft Clarity** (Microsoft Corporation) to understand how visitors interact with our pages through heatmaps and session replays. Cookies: `_clck` (up to 1 year), `_clsk` (1 day), and Microsoft cookies such as `CLID` and `MUID`. Clarity is configured to mask text you type, and it does not run inside the client dashboard.

These tools only load after you click **Accept** in our cookie banner. We have turned off advertising features: we do not use this data for advertising or ad personalization, and Google signals are disabled.

**Changing your choice.** You can accept or decline analytics cookies at any time using the **Cookie preferences** button on this page. If you decline, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity stop loading on our site and we delete the analytics cookies stored under our domain. Cookies that Microsoft stores under its own domains (such as `clarity.ms` and `bing.com`) can only be removed through your browser settings; they are not used by our site after you decline. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, or install the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

To learn more, see [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

**Embedded content from other services.** Some pages include content provided by other companies, which they may use to collect information and set cookies under their own privacy policies, independently of your choice above:

- Our **newsletter sign-up form** is provided by [beehiiv](https://www.beehiiv.com/privacy), which uses its own analytics and bot-protection tools inside the form.
- **Videos** are hosted by YouTube and only load when you choose to play one. We use YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode.
- **Meeting booking** is provided by [HubSpot](https://legal.hubspot.com/privacy-policy) when you choose to schedule a call.

### How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, or as required by law. Analytics data is kept according to the retention settings of each tool.

### How we protect information

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information, including encryption in transit, access controls, two-step verification for staff and partners, and audit logging of access to client files.

### Your rights

You can ask to access the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent, by contacting our Privacy Officer. We will respond within the time required by law.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or the privacy regulator in your province, such as the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia or the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

### Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.
